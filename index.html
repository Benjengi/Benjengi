A conceptual Earth-observation satellite project exploring how an optical payload can be packaged, accessed and integrated within a compact spacecraft structure.

Project scope +

Engineering problem

Balance limited internal space with payload access, camera clearance and manufacturable structural interfaces.

Development scope

Research into structure, materials, interfaces, solar-panel arrangement and subsystem packaging. Planned verification includes CAD interference checks, thermal FEA and educational structural load cases.

Intended deliverables

CAD assembly, payload subassembly, part and assembly drawings, supporting calculations and a design report.
